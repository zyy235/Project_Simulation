Thank you for playing this game.

Project: Simulation is an incremental game where you collect 
resources and progress.

-----Pre-alpha stage
Here, Amplifiers(A1-A8) produce Multipliers(M1-M8).
Intensifiers(I1-I8) empower Multiplier production.
Your matter gain rate is based on the product of your
multipliers empowered to an amount based on your
Global Instability Magnitude(GIM).

Prestige is enabled after obtaining an A3. Doing it can decrease your
GIM. More matter means a further reduction. After prestige once,
Prestige Upgrades(PU) unlock. You can buy them to push your progress
further.

-----Alpha
So you have 1.8e308 matter and you're ready for performing an Alpha.
You then unlock a new tab "Alpha", which includes 3 subtabs:
Alpha Upgrades, Break Alpha Upgrades and Alpha Milestones.

Alpha Upgrades(AU) is where you spend matter/Aeros/AP to buy
Alpha Upgrade Points(AUP), and you spend them on various upgrades that
helps you push the progress.

Alpha Milestones is where you check the milestones you have.
You unlock milestones by reaching certain Alpha, which can be obtained from
performing Alpha. Most of them are QoLs.

Break Alpha Upgrades(BAUs) are upgrades that are available after breaking Alpha.
By breaking Alpha, you can get more AP based on matter and many other factors
in one run.

At one point, you unlock another Amp./Int. System related to Alpha.

Current goal is to reach the world's limit.

-----Hotkeys
A + 1: Buy Amplifier 1 (etc.)
I + 1: Buy Intensifier 1 (etc.)
M: Buy all
Caps + P: Prestige once.
P + 1: Buy Prestige Upgrade 1 (etc.)
Shift + A: Buy Amp. Aeromagnifier
Shift + I: Buy Amp. Aeromagnifier
Caps + A: Alpha once.
